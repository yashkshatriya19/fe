How Does a Dice Work?

A standard dice has six sides, and each side contains a different number from 1 to 6. When the dice is rolled, one of the six numbers appears on top.

Watch the Dice Video

In this video, you can see a dice being rolled from different angles. The video demonstrates how the dice moves and eventually stops with one number facing upward.

Conclusion

Dice are simple but useful objects that are used in many games. Rolling a dice provides a quick way to generate a random number between 1 and 6.
